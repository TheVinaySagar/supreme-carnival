*   **Momentum:** The MACD and MACD signal lines are crucial. Look for crossovers of the MACD line above the signal line as potential buy signals, and vice versa for sell signals. Also, watch for divergence between the MACD and the price, which can signal potential trend reversals. The RSI will provide additional insight into overbought and oversold conditions.
*   **Volatility:** The ATR will indicate the average volatility over the past period. This is very important for setting appropriate stop-loss levels and determining position sizes. The Bollinger Bands will show potential breakout zones and areas of potential reversals.
*   **Volume Confirmation:** Analyze the volume data in conjunction with the price action. High volume on up days confirms buying pressure, while high volume on down days confirms selling pressure. The VWMA could be used to confirm the trend by integrating price action with volume data.
